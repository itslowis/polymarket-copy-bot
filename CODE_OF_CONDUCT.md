# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone participating in this project — filing issues, opening pull requests, commenting, reviewing — is expected to treat others with respect. We want participation to be free of harassment regardless of experience level, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- **Deliberately encouraging others to take financial risks they have not understood.** This project moves real money. Do not pressure anyone into running it with funds they cannot afford to lose, and do not present it as a reliable source of profit.
- **Submitting code intended to steal funds or credentials.** Contributing anything designed to exfiltrate a private key, redirect trades, or weaken a safety limit without disclosure will result in an immediate and permanent ban, and will be reported to GitHub.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit history — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
